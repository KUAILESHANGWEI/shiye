拾页隐私政策

更新日期 2026年11月14日

资料保存与 iCloud 同步

你保存的网站、书签、笔记、图片及文件存储在设备的应用资料库中。登录 Apple 账户并启用 iCloud 后，资料通过 Apple CloudKit 的私人数据库在同一 iCloud 账户的设备之间同步。同步需要网络及可用的 iCloud 服务，可能受系统后台调度影响。开发者无法读取其他用户的私人 CloudKit 数据库。

链接信息与第三方网站

当你读取链接标题、介绍或图片，或打开收藏链接时，应用会联系该链接所属的网站。YouTube 和 X 的部分链接信息可能通过对应平台的公开接口读取。相关平台可能按各自政策处理网络请求、IP 地址及访问记录。应用会展示这些网站返回的信息。

图片、文件及权限

应用处理你主动选择、粘贴或拍摄的图片和文件。系统文件选择器、照片选择器及相机提供所需访问权限。你可以通过系统设置调整相关权限。

密码与备份

开机密码和删除密码的校验信息保存在当前设备。导出的加密备份由你设置的独立备份密码保护。应用内开机密码用于界面访问验证，设备资料还依赖 Apple 平台的沙箱与系统数据保护。请妥善保管备份密码，开发者无法代为恢复。

数据收集、广告与统计

拾页未集成广告或第三方行为分析 SDK，开发者不通过应用建立用户账号，也不收集收藏内容、设备标识或使用记录用于广告或跟踪。Apple 可能依照其政策处理 App Store、TestFlight、iCloud 及系统诊断数据。

删除与保留

你可以在应用内删除收藏。启用同步时，删除操作会传播到同一 iCloud 账户的其他设备。离线设备需要在重新联网后处理同步。系统备份、你导出的备份以及分享出去的文件由你和相应存储服务管理。

技术支持与政策更新

关于隐私、数据处理或故障的问题，可通过技术支持页面联系开发者。公开技术支持邮箱将在正式发布前补充。政策变动将在本页面更新。

Shiye Privacy Policy

Updated November 14, 2026

Storage and iCloud sync

Saved websites, bookmarks, notes, images and files are stored in the app library on your device. When you sign in to your Apple Account and enable iCloud, Apple CloudKit private databases synchronize the library between devices using the same iCloud account. Sync requires a network connection and available iCloud service, and timing depends on system background scheduling. The developer cannot read other users’ private CloudKit databases.

Links and third-party websites

Reading a link’s title, description or image, or opening a saved link, contacts the relevant website. Some YouTube and X metadata may be obtained through the platform’s public endpoints. Those services may process requests, IP addresses and access logs under their own policies. The app displays information returned by those websites.

Images, files and permissions

The app processes images and files you choose, paste or capture. System file and photo pickers and the camera provide the required access. You can adjust applicable permissions in system settings.

Passwords and backups

Verification information for launch and deletion passwords stays on the current device. Exported encrypted backups use the separate backup password you choose. The launch password controls access to the app interface. Local data also relies on Apple platform sandboxing and system data protection. Keep your backup password safe, as the developer cannot recover it.

Collection, advertising and analytics

Shiye includes no advertising or third-party behavioral analytics SDK. The developer does not create app accounts or collect saved content, device identifiers or usage records for advertising or tracking. Apple may process App Store, TestFlight, iCloud and system diagnostics data under its own policies.

Deletion and retention

You can delete items in the app. With sync enabled, deletion propagates to other devices using the same iCloud account. Offline devices process sync after reconnecting. System backups, exported backups and shared files are managed by you and the relevant storage service.

Support and policy updates

Contact the developer through the support page for privacy, data handling or technical questions. A public support email will be added before release. Policy changes will be posted on this page.
